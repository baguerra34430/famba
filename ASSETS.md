# Asset provenance

`famba-emblem.webp` is the redesigned emblem, generated from the owner's logo at the owner's
request. It retains the globe and growth motif in navy, teal and muted gold on white.
`famba-logo.png` retains the original supplied logo for reference and is not deployed.

`trade-harbour.webp` is an original AI-generated editorial illustration of an imagined
Mediterranean commercial harbour. It does not depict an identified real port or a FAMBA project.
Both generated assets were converted to WebP for efficient delivery without visual alterations.

The photographs were inspected before integration. Except for the new Tangier photograph,
their source URLs came from the owner's earlier website. They illustrate markets and subjects;
they do not document FAMBA events or members. CSS applies consistent crops and subtle desaturation.
