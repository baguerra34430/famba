Who is FAMBA-SMEs?

A practical international business association built to help SMEs connect, enter new markets and create durable commercial partnerships.

A bridge for business.

We connect companies, entrepreneurs, institutions and local networks across France, the Maghreb, China and the Gulf. We turn introductions into partnerships, investment projects, trade flows and market-entry opportunities.

Our Mission

Create trusted commercial bridges, facilitate market access and help SMEs turn international ambition into practical business.

Our Vision

Build a durable North–South business corridor where French, Maghreb, Chinese and Gulf companies grow together through investment and cooperation.
